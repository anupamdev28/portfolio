## Chosen Direction — Orbiting Workshop

### Design Movement
Neo-futurist product studio with references to kinetic identity systems, generative interface art, and premium creative technology portfolios.

### Core Principles
1. **Make the work feel alive:** every major section should have a sense of orbit, depth, or responsive movement.
2. **Show range without noise:** services are organized as a clear constellation rather than a generic list.
3. **Contrast precision with warmth:** sharp type and technical labels are softened by amber light, frosted surfaces, and human copy.
4. **Keep the next action obvious:** every scroll chapter ends with a simple invitation to collaborate.

### Color Philosophy
The base is midnight graphite, chosen to make the interface feel like a focused studio after hours. A warm signal amber (#FFB86B) becomes the ownable brand color: it reads like an active node, a lit workspace, and a human presence inside a technical system. Pale mint and cool blue are reserved for secondary system states so the amber remains the emotional anchor.

### Layout Paradigm
Use a left-anchored narrative rail with offset content panels, floating annotations, and full-bleed depth fields. Avoid symmetrical centered landing-page blocks. The hero uses a split composition: an editorial text column and a large interactive-looking orbital object on the right; later sections alternate horizontal rhythm and staggered cards.

### Signature Elements
- Amber “signal node” dots connected by thin orbital traces.
- Frosted glass panels with subtle grain and technical coordinates.
- Oversized italic display numerals for chapter markers and service indexes.

### Interaction Philosophy
Hover states should feel like inspecting a live system: cards lift slightly, the amber node brightens, and supporting metadata slides into view. Navigation is calm and quick; motion is reserved for depth, discovery, and the transition between chapters.

### Animation
Use transform-and-opacity-only motion. The orbital centerpiece drifts in a slow, barely noticeable loop; signal nodes pulse on a 3–4 second rhythm. Section reveals use a 40–60ms stagger and a strong ease-out. Buttons press to 0.97 scale. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by freezing drift and disabling non-essential entrance movement.

### Typography System
Display: **Space Grotesk**, 700–600, tight tracking for names and section headlines. Body: **DM Sans**, 400–500, for readable descriptions and navigation. Technical metadata: **IBM Plex Mono**, 500, uppercase with generous tracking. Use italic Space Grotesk selectively for human emphasis, not for every heading.

### Brand Essence
Anupam Dev helps ambitious builders turn rough ideas into websites, apps, SaaS products, AI agents, and growth systems — from the perspective of a hands-on B.Tech student building in public. Personality: **curious, systems-minded, energetic**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines sound direct, observant, and slightly cinematic. CTAs sound like an invitation to build, not a sales pitch. Microcopy is specific and human.

Example lines:
- “I build the layer between a sharp idea and a working product.”
- “Bring the messy brief. I’ll bring the system.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a custom “AD” signal mark: two offset amber arcs forming an abstract orbital monogram, paired with the wordmark “ANUPAM DEV” in Space Grotesk with a deliberate split in the letter spacing. The mark should remain recognizable as a standalone favicon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Amber — #FFB86B**. It is warm, active, and ownable against the midnight graphite foundation.

## Style Decisions

- Services are presented as a connected constellation of inspectable modules with a vertical trace, active nodes, and system-map annotation.
- Signal Amber #FFB86B is reserved for active system energy: primary actions, live nodes, key numerals, and human italic emphasis.
- Each major chapter includes an orbit, depth field, or technical annotation so the Orbiting Workshop language persists beyond the hero.
